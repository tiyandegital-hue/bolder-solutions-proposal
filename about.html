About

A Dawson City
contractor.

No founding myth, no invented milestones — just the facts.

Bolder Solutions is a Dawson City, Yukon business providing heavy equipment mechanical services and versatile earthmoving solutions. It's run by owner Kyle Isaac, and it's listed in the Tr'ondëk Hwëch'in Government business directory — checkable by anyone, any time.

The work is straightforward: keep machines running, move earth and materials where they need to go, and show up when the job is scheduled. The fleet — a Kubota SVL75 skid steer, a 16′ dumping trailer, a 20′ tilt-deck trailer and a flat-deck Dodge 1-ton — is based in Dawson City, and additional machinery is brought in as projects require.

When you call 867-335-2477, you reach Kyle directly. That's the whole structure: one accountable person, real equipment, local to the Klondike.
