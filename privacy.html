Privacy policy.

Plain language, no fine print games.

What we collect

The quote form on this site asks for your name, phone number, optional email, the service you need, project details and a preferred date. That's it — nothing else is collected through forms.

Why we collect it

To prepare your quote and contact you about it. Nothing you enter is used for marketing, sold, or shared with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The quote form works entirely in your browser; the request is only sent when you choose to send it by email.

Third-party services

None are loaded by this site — no embedded maps, fonts, videos or trackers. If you click a link that opens your phone app or email client, that app's own policies apply.

Your rights

Ask us what information we hold about you, and we'll tell you — and delete it on request. Contact kyle@boldersolutions.ca or 867-335-2477.
