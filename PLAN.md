# Plan — Bolder Solutions proposal site (2026-11-03)

## What the client actually needs
Bolder Solutions: real Dawson City, Yukon business (owner Kyle Isaac) with heavy
equipment and no website (directory listing confirms Website column is blank).
The site IS the pitch — it shows what their website would look like.

## Psychology brief
- Visitor: Dawson/Klondike property owners, placer operators, builders — anyone
  who needs dirt moved or a machine fixed in a remote town.
- Pain: machines break, downtime is expensive, reliable operators are hard to find.
- One action: call or request a quote.
- Trust gap: invisible business with no web presence — must look established, local, capable.
- Emotion: rugged frontier competence — "the machine is already running."

## Mood in three words + one signature idea per page
Mood: rugged, premium, frontier.
- index: giant outlined ghost "YUKON" behind a condensed-type hero + hazard-stripe dividers
- services: numbered editorial list (01–04) with amber numerals
- fleet: spec tables for the four REAL machines
- service-area: styled SVG coverage panel (no third-party map)
- about: plain facts, no myth — the honesty page
- quote: the conversion peak — labeled, keyboard-friendly form building a copy-ready brief
- contact: tap-to-call vcard grid

## Pages (9, multipage — never a one-pager)
1. index.html — Home (hero, services strip, honest trust band, fleet preview, service-area band, final CTA)
2. services.html — 4 numbered services + process + [TO CONFIRM] block
3. fleet.html — 4 real machines as spec tables + to-confirm for years/capacities
4. service-area.html — Dawson base + Klondike/Yukon reach, SVG panel, mobilization notes
5. about.html — verified facts only, no founding story
6. quote.html — labeled form (autocomplete, visible errors, consent) → copy-ready brief + mailto
7. contact.html — real phone/email/address, tap-to-call
8. privacy.html, 9. terms.html — short standard pages

## Tokens (offline-safe)
--bg #16130F, --surface #241D15, --ink #F4EFE6, --muted #B7AB94, --amber #F5A623
(condensed display stack: Arial Narrow / Bahnschrift / Franklin Gothic / Futura).
No webfonts, no external assets, no cookies, no analytics — no consent banner needed.

## Honesty law compliance
No invented stats, testimonials, years in business, licenses or prices anywhere.
Unknown facts use dashed [TO CONFIRM WITH KYLE] blocks — visible on the page, not hidden.
Fleet = directory facts only; photos are original inline SVG line-art (no copyright risk).
